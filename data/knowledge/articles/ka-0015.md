# Evidence-led Initial Triage: Office Workflow

**Article ID:** KA-0015

## Category
Other

## Symptoms
workflow interruption; unexpected behavior

## Environment
ResolveAI synthetic enterprise simulation only.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Confirm the workflow interruption symptom, affected synthetic office, and observed time window.
2. Compare Service Desk behavior from an approved second synthetic device.
3. Capture safe status and latency evidence before changing the simulated service path.

## Resolution Guidance
Restore the approved Service Desk access path and verify the original workflow.

## Escalation Criteria
Escalate when two or more synthetic users share the symptom or errors persist.
