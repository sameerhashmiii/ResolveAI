# Intermittent Lookup Evidence: Remote Workflow

**Article ID:** KA-0072

## Category
DNS / Name Resolution

## Symptoms
slow hostname lookup; internal names unavailable

## Environment
ResolveAI synthetic enterprise simulation only.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Confirm the slow hostname lookup symptom, affected synthetic office, and observed time window.
2. Compare DNS behavior from an approved second synthetic device.
3. Capture safe status and latency evidence before changing the simulated service path.

## Resolution Guidance
Refresh the supported DNS session, then record before-and-after evidence.

## Escalation Criteria
Escalate with timestamps when availability or latency breaches the simulated target.
