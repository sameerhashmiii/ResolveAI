# Unstable Remote Sessions: Office Workflow

**Article ID:** KA-0016

## Category
VPN / Remote Access

## Symptoms
gateway rejection; remote disconnect

## Environment
ResolveAI synthetic enterprise simulation only.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Confirm the remote disconnect symptom, affected synthetic office, and observed time window.
2. Compare VPN behavior from an approved second synthetic device.
3. Capture safe status and latency evidence before changing the simulated service path.

## Resolution Guidance
Refresh the supported VPN session, then record before-and-after evidence.

## Escalation Criteria
Escalate with timestamps when availability or latency breaches the simulated target.
