# Internal Hostname Verification: Remote Workflow

**Article ID:** KA-0057

## Category
DNS / Name Resolution

## Symptoms
slow hostname lookup; internal names unavailable

## Environment
ResolveAI synthetic enterprise simulation only.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Confirm the internal names unavailable symptom, affected synthetic office, and observed time window.
2. Compare DNS behavior from an approved second synthetic device.
3. Capture safe status and latency evidence before changing the simulated service path.

## Resolution Guidance
Restore the approved DNS access path and verify the original workflow.

## Escalation Criteria
Escalate when two or more synthetic users share the symptom or errors persist.
