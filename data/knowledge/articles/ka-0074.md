# Sign-in Evidence Review: Remote Workflow

**Article ID:** KA-0074

## Category
Security Alerts

## Symptoms
policy warning; sign-in risk prompt

## Environment
ResolveAI synthetic enterprise simulation only.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Confirm the policy warning symptom, affected synthetic office, and observed time window.
2. Compare Active Directory behavior from an approved second synthetic device.
3. Capture safe status and latency evidence before changing the simulated service path.

## Resolution Guidance
Refresh the supported Active Directory session, then record before-and-after evidence.

## Escalation Criteria
Escalate with timestamps when availability or latency breaches the simulated target.
