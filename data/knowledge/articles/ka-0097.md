# Internal Path Verification: Multi-user Evidence

**Article ID:** KA-0097

## Category
Network Connectivity

## Symptoms
intermittent path; internal timeout

## Environment
ResolveAI synthetic enterprise simulation only.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Confirm the intermittent path symptom, affected synthetic office, and observed time window.
2. Compare Network behavior from an approved second synthetic device.
3. Capture safe status and latency evidence before changing the simulated service path.

## Resolution Guidance
Restore the approved Network access path and verify the original workflow.

## Escalation Criteria
Escalate when two or more synthetic users share the symptom or errors persist.
