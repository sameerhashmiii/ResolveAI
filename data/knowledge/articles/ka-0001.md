# Gateway Sign-in Verification: Office Workflow

**Article ID:** KA-0001

## Category
VPN / Remote Access

## Symptoms
gateway rejection; remote disconnect

## Environment
ResolveAI synthetic enterprise simulation only.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Confirm the gateway rejection symptom, affected synthetic office, and observed time window.
2. Compare VPN behavior from an approved second synthetic device.
3. Capture safe status and latency evidence before changing the simulated service path.

## Resolution Guidance
Restore the approved VPN access path and verify the original workflow.

## Escalation Criteria
Escalate when two or more synthetic users share the symptom or errors persist.
