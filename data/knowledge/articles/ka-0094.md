# Application Sign-in Evidence: Multi-user Evidence

**Article ID:** KA-0094

## Category
Application Access

## Symptoms
application timeout; access denied

## Environment
ResolveAI synthetic enterprise simulation only.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Confirm the application timeout symptom, affected synthetic office, and observed time window.
2. Compare PayrollPro behavior from an approved second synthetic device.
3. Capture safe status and latency evidence before changing the simulated service path.

## Resolution Guidance
Restore the approved PayrollPro access path and verify the original workflow.

## Escalation Criteria
Escalate when two or more synthetic users share the symptom or errors persist.
