## Category
Account Lockout

## Symptoms
locked account; sign-in refused

## Environment
ResolveAI synthetic enterprise simulation only.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Confirm the sign-in refused symptom, affected synthetic office, and observed time window.
2. Compare Active Directory behavior from an approved second synthetic device.
3. Capture safe status and latency evidence before changing the simulated service path.

## Resolution Guidance
Apply the documented synthetic configuration and confirm multi-user recovery.

## Escalation Criteria
Escalate when safe comparison tests indicate a service-wide interruption.
