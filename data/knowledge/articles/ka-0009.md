# Lockout Verification: Office Workflow

**Article ID:** KA-0009

## Category
Account Lockout

## Symptoms
locked account; sign-in refused

## Environment
ResolveAI synthetic enterprise simulation only.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Confirm the locked account symptom, affected synthetic office, and observed time window.
2. Compare Active Directory behavior from an approved second synthetic device.
3. Capture safe status and latency evidence before changing the simulated service path.

## Resolution Guidance
Restore the approved Active Directory access path and verify the original workflow.

## Escalation Criteria
Escalate when two or more synthetic users share the symptom or errors persist.

## Related Systems
Active Directory
